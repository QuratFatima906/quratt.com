## The brief

An online store for a Pakistani jewellery brand: one seller, ready stock only, shipping within Pakistan, paid with JazzCash, Easypaisa or cash on delivery. The running costs had to stay around $50 a month.

## How I scoped it

Before the code, an 800-line spec built from a Q&A with the owner. Sizes are the only variants, checkout works as a guest, shipping is a flat rate, and discounts and reviews are in the first version. Just as important is the long list of what is **not** in it.

- **One Next.js app, not a monorepo**, with a custom admin and four roles enforced in more than one layer.
- **15 Postgres tables**, including an inventory audit trail, because stock disputes are the support tickets a small brand cannot afford.
- **14 build phases, each with a "done when" check.** Phase 6 launches with cash on delivery only, so a slow payment-gateway onboarding cannot hold the launch hostage.
- **A cost model before a stack:** $21–46 a month, all in.
